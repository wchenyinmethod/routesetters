How to climb

Your two arms

The mouse is where you are reaching. Both arms follow the cursor. Hold left mouse to close your left hand, hold right mouse to close your right. Let go of the button and the hand opens. If a hold is within reach when you close your hand, you latch onto it.

On a trackpad, Q and E do the same thing.

Pulling up

This is the whole trick. Once a hand is on a hold, move the mouse above that hold and the arm contracts, hauling your body upward. Move the mouse below it and the arm pays out and you hang long.

So a move is: latch, pull the mouse up, throw the free hand at the next hold, close that hand, release the first one.

Feet and momentum

Your feet look after themselves. They find footholds when they are near one and you are not thrashing about. Standing on something halves how fast your arms tire.

Space kicks off your feet for a dyno. Use it for gaps you cannot reach.

Grip and stamina

The GRIP bar is your forearms. Crimps drain it fast, jugs barely touch it, rest ledges refill it. At zero your hands open whether you like it or not.

Holds also slip. If you are swinging, or the hold is greasy, wet or iced, it will spit you off. The hold shakes and goes red just before it does.

Falling

There is no death. You slide down the wall, land badly, and start again from your last quickdraw clip, or from the ground if nobody has set one.

Party mode

Each round you get three cards and place one. Then everyone climbs the wall in turn with a 75 second clock. Topping out is one point, three if you are the only one who did it, and there is a bonus for touching the golden Party Box if someone has set one.

The good cards help everyone, including your rivals. That is the game.
